phlite

Persistent homology in Rust: lazy, so it only builds the parts of a boundary matrix the reduction needs; fast when it does build them; with Python bindings, and a playground that runs in your browser.

Playground

Compute barcodes of point clouds, images, weighted networks and chain complexes, from examples, random data, your own files or by drawing. See the representatives on your data. Everything runs locally, on all your cores.
